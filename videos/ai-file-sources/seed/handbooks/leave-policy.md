# Contoso leave policy

## Annual leave

Every full-time Contoso employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is requested in the
staff portal, and a manager answers within two working days.

## Sick leave

Employees can take up to 10 paid sick days per year without a doctor's note. A note is needed after three days in a row.

## Parental leave

New parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave, which can be taken any time during the first year.
