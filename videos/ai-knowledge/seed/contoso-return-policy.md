# Contoso return policy

Contoso wants every customer to be happy with a purchase. This policy explains how returns and refunds work.

## Returning an item

You can return most unused items within 45 days of the delivery date. The item must be in its original packaging.
Start a return from the Orders page of your account, and print the prepaid return label.

## Refunds

Contoso refunds the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item arrives at the warehouse.
Shipping costs are refunded when the item arrived damaged or was not what you ordered.

## Items with a shorter window

Headphones and other personal care items can be returned within 14 days, and only when they are unopened.
Gift cards cannot be returned.

## Exchanges

To exchange an item for a different size or color, return it and place a new order. The new order ships right away,
so you do not have to wait for the refund.
